# LineGuide v2.3 — iPhone test checklist

Use **fictional data only**. Export your current library JSON backup before updating. Do not clear Safari / Home Screen app data.

1. Open LineGuide and confirm **Relevant-choice build v2.3** on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Create**. Choose **Other — enter your own** for Task type. Confirm the text box appears. Enter a custom task type.
3. For Site, Line and Asset, choose **Other**, enter fictional values, and proceed. Use Back and Continue to confirm your entries remain intact.
4. On Requirements, expand Prerequisites, Hazards and PPE. Select a normal choice plus **Other** in each section. Enter two custom choices on different lines in one of the sections.
5. Go Back and forward again. Confirm all checked items and custom values remain.
6. Try checking **Other** with a blank custom field. Continue should be refused with a clear prompt.
7. Add a procedure step. For Step category and Verification approach, choose Other and enter sample wording. Complete action, expected result, acceptance checks and document control.
8. Save. Inspect the finished document: your custom entries must appear in the correct sections.
9. Reopen a previously created procedure; confirm older choices are still displayed, even if the name isn't in the new short list.
10. Export a JSON backup. Verify that the custom entries are in the saved document (and preserve a copy externally).

If the old version persists after publishing, refresh the page while online and reopen the Home Screen app. Do **not** clear stored website data to force an update.

## Scope warning

The menus are prompts, not a risk assessment. Machine-specific controls, OEM information, acceptance criteria, instructions and approval require competent verification. Named approval fields remain unauthenticated in the current personal prototype.
